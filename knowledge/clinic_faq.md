## About Arogya Clinic

Arogya Clinic is a general practice clinic in Koramangala, Bangalore. We were founded in 2018 with a mission to provide accessible, honest primary healthcare for local families. We are not a hospital and we do not offer emergency services. We care for around three hundred regular patients and focus on continuity of care, clear communication, and practical advice you can follow at home.

## Location and how to find us

Our address is 45, 5th Cross, Koramangala 4th Block, Bangalore 560034. We are near the Koramangala water tank and opposite the BBMP park. The Koramangala 4th Block BMTC bus stop is about two minutes away on foot. Autos and cabs are easy to find on 5th Cross. On Google Maps, search for Arogya Clinic Koramangala to get directions to the building entrance.

## Clinic hours

For clinic timings, opening hours, and when we are open: we are open Monday to Saturday from nine in the morning to one in the afternoon, and from five in the evening to eight at night. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays. The last appointment is accepted thirty minutes before closing time. Walk-ins are welcome when slots are available, but waiting time may be longer than for patients who have booked in advance.

## Booking and cancellation policy

Appointments are thirty minutes long. Please arrive five minutes early. Cancel at least two hours before your appointment time — we cannot accommodate late cancellations. Rescheduling is free once. A second reschedule within the same week incurs a small admin charge of one hundred rupees. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability on the day.

## Consultation fees

A general consultation costs five hundred rupees. A follow-up visit for the same condition within two weeks costs three hundred and fifty rupees. A specialist consultation costs eight hundred rupees. Home visits are not currently available.

## Payments we accept

We accept cash, UPI including Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe, and BHIM, and debit and credit cards. For health insurance, we offer cashless settlement for Star Health Insurance, Niva Bupa which was formerly Max Bupa, and HDFC Ergo. For all other insurers, patients pay at the clinic and claim reimbursement from their provider. Please confirm your coverage before your visit.

## Lab and diagnostic services

We offer in-clinic lab tests including complete blood count, blood sugar fasting and random, HbA1c, lipid profile, thyroid panel covering T3 T4 and TSH, liver function tests, kidney function tests, and urine analysis. Most results are available the same day by six in the evening. HbA1c results are ready the following morning. We do not have X-ray, ECG, or ultrasound on-site — we will refer you to a nearby diagnostic centre if needed.

## Pharmacy

We have a small in-clinic pharmacy stocking common medicines, first-aid supplies, and over-the-counter medications. For a full-service pharmacy, MedPlus is a two-minute walk on 4th Cross. Apollo Pharmacy is about five minutes away on Koramangala 1st Block.

## Parking

Free basement parking is available in the building. Tell the building security you are visiting Arogya Clinic. Two-wheeler parking is available at the building entrance. Street parking is also available along 5th Cross when basement spaces are full.

## Emergencies and urgent care

Arogya Clinic is not an emergency facility. For any medical emergency, call 108 immediately or go to Manipal Hospital on Old Airport Road or St. John's Medical College Hospital on Sarjapur Road. For urgent but non-emergency concerns, call us first on our clinic number and we will advise whether you should come in or go to hospital.
